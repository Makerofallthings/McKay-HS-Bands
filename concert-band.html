Concert Band.

The ensemble

Learn.
Then grow louder.

Concert Band is a non-auditioned group for students who are here to learn and get better. The ensemble performs four times a year and its members also participate in Pep Band.

Students build tone, technique, rehearsal habits, and the confidence to contribute inside a full concert ensemble.

At a glance

  • PerformsFour concert performances each year
  • Also inPep Band
