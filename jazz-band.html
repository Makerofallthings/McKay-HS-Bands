Jazz Band.

The ensemble

Know the form.
Then say something.

Jazz Band is an auditioned group of freshmen through seniors. The ensemble travels to jazz festivals across the region and competes for a place in the State Jazz Championships.

Students are also required to participate in a concert-performing group—Concert Band, Symphonic Band, or Wind Ensemble. Some members join a Jazz Combo with a focused emphasis on improvisation.
