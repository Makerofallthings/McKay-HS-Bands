Symphonic Band.

The ensemble

Symphonic Band is an auditioned group of students ranging from freshmen to seniors. Its members also contribute to Marching Band, Pep Band, and Jazz Band.

The ensemble creates a bridge between developing concert musicianship and the demands of McKay’s most advanced performance settings.
