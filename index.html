The McKay sound

Discipline meets discovery.

The McKay High School Band Program serves student musicians, families, faculty, and the wider Salem community through concert, jazz, marching, and percussion experiences.

Under the direction of Scott Avzaradel, students perform standard band and jazz repertoire alongside music from film, stage, and contemporary traditions—building craft and community in equal measure.

Director of Bands

Scott
Avzaradel.

Scott Avzaradel leads more than 180 McKay musicians across concert, jazz, marching, orchestra, pep, and pit settings. He founded the McKay Jazz Festival, helped found the Salem-Keizer All-City Jazz Band, and also teaches at Willamette University and Western Oregon University.
