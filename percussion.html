Percussion.

The course

Time is
everything.

Percussion is open to students who have successfully auditioned for the Marching Band drum line. Enrollment is required for drum line members, with rehearsals and performances outside class time.

The course covers both marching band and concert band material and is required as a co-enrollment for percussion students on the Marching Band drum line.
