Wind Ensemble is McKay’s top auditioned group, made up of students from freshmen through seniors. Members also participate in Marching Band, Pep Band, Jazz Ensemble, Symphony Orchestra, musical pit ensembles, and chamber groups outside of class.

The group has built a sustained record of qualifying for the OSAA State Championship—and earned the 2025 5A Band title.
