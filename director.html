Director of Bands

A program built
across rooms.

Scott Avzaradel directs Wind Ensemble, Symphonic Band, Concert Band, Jazz 1–2, Jazz Combo, Marching Band, Pep Band, and Pit Orchestra at McKay High School. Across those settings, he leads more than 180 students.

He is the founder of the McKay Jazz Festival and a founder of the Salem-Keizer All-City Jazz Band. Beyond McKay, Avzaradel teaches at Willamette University and Western Oregon University and maintains a private studio for trumpet and low-brass musicians.

The Oregon Symphony named Avzaradel a 2025/26 Salem Music Educator of the Year nominee. The recognition followed McKay’s 2025 OSAA 5A Band State Championship under his direction.
